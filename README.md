# Pace Tool

A running calculator for pacing, distances, and race splits. Works offline on any device.

🔗 **Try it now**: [pace-tool](https://joneholland.github.io/pace-tool/)

---

## Pace Converter

Convert your running pace between min/km and min/mi instantly. Set your pace using the scroll wheel, and see:

- **Converted pace** in the other unit
- **Predicted finish times** for 7 race distances — from 5K to 50K

Supported race distances: 5K, 10K, 15K, 10 Mile, Half Marathon, Marathon, 50K

---

## Distance Converter

Convert any distance between kilometers and miles with precision down to 0.01.

Includes a quick reference table showing all common race distances in both units.

---

## Split Calculator

Plan your race with customizable split strategies:

1. **Pick your race** — 5K through 50K
2. **Enter your target time**
3. **Choose your strategy** — positive splits (start fast, finish slow), even splits, or negative splits (start slow, finish fast)

The calculator generates a detailed breakdown showing the pace and time for each kilometer or mile, plus your cumulative time throughout the race.

---

## Install on Your Phone

Add Pace Tool to your home screen for instant access — no app store required.

**iPhone/iPad (Safari)**
1. Tap the Share button
2. Tap "Add to Home Screen"

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Tap the menu (⋮)
2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"

Once installed, Pace Tool works completely offline — even in airplane mode.
